## **Overview of the Thesis**

Solo dining has become increasingly common among young individuals due to academic schedules, busy lifestyles, or personal preference. Although convenient, eating alone may lead to feelings of isolation, reduced meal enjoyment, and diminished emotional engagement during dining.

Our thesis, **“Enhancing Emotional Companionship Among Young Solo Diners with Mobile-Based Artificial Eating Companion,”** introduces a mobile solution designed to improve the emotional experience of young solo diners.

At the core of our system is **KainBuddy 2.0**, an interactive virtual companion presented through a **video-call-like interface**. Users can open the app while eating, and KainBuddy 2.0 provides:

- Warm, friendly conversation  
- Emotional responsiveness  
- Encouraging prompts for mindful eating  
- A sense of presence and companionship  

The purpose of this system is to **enhance emotional companionship** and create a more enjoyable dining experience for individuals who frequently eat alone.
